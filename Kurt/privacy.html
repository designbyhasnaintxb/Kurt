Privacy

What this site collects

The only personal information this website collects is what you choose to send through the lesson request form:

  • your name and email address
  • your phone number and preferred days / times, if you add them
  • who the coaching is for, your current experience, the areas you'd like to improve, and any message you write

How it's used

Your details are used only to reply to your request about private pickleball coaching. They are not sold or shared for marketing.

Where it's stored

Form submissions are received and stored by the website's hosting provider (Netlify) so they can be passed on to Coach Leon.

Fonts and scripts

The site loads fonts from Google Fonts and animation libraries from public content-delivery networks, which receive standard technical information (such as your IP address) when your browser requests those files.

Changes or removal

If you'd like your details corrected or removed, send a note through the lesson request form and mention it in your message.
