Meet Coach Leon

Meet Coach Leon Thomas.

Leon is a pickleball coach originally from Jamaica, with nearly ten years of experience coaching children and adults of different ages and skill levels.

Most of that experience was developed in Jamaica. Now in New Jersey, Leon is building his coaching presence here — offering private, one-on-one lessons for players who want personal attention and a clear place to start.

Approachable, beginner friendly, and passionate about teaching the game.

Court size

A pickleball court is 44 × 20 ft, with a 7 ft non-volley zone — the kitchen — on each side of the net. Shown here against a 78 × 36 ft tennis court.
