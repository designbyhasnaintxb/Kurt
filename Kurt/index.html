Meet Coach Kurt

From hockey dad to two decades of coaching.

Hockey was part of Kurt's life from an early age. Years later, coaching his own children brought him back into the game from a new perspective.

What started as helping his kids developed into nearly two decades of coaching young players. Today, his focus is helping developing athletes build skills, confidence and a stronger foundation on the ice.

A coach who knows the game — and knows the view from the parent's side of the glass.
